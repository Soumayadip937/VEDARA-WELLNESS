About Vedara

Hospitality understanding. Wellness expertise.

Vedara Wellness is built to operate as a professional wellness partner within hotels and resorts, creating a calm, refined and consistent guest experience.

Our approach combines experienced spa professionals with strong understanding of hotel operations, financial discipline and guest-service expectations.

Hotel Industry ExpertiseAlmost 8 years of hospitality experience, with focus on financial and operational understanding.
Spa Industry ExpertiseAn experienced team with approximately 10 years of hands-on spa and massage experience.

Our vision

To become a trusted wellness partner for leading hotels and resorts, creating exceptional spa experiences that become an integral part of modern hospitality.

Our mission

To deliver professional, guest-centric wellness experiences while creating a sustainable and mutually beneficial partnership for the hotel.
